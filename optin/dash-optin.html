How to opt in

Participants opt in by texting +1 (507) 431-7807 to begin. The first message in every conversation is sent by the participant. There is no list to join, no phone number to submit anywhere on this page, and nothing to agree to before the number is shown — it is published above.

By texting +1 (507) 431-7807, you agree to receive text messages from the Child Mind Institute relating to the DASH interviewer pilot. Message frequency varies; one session runs 30 to 60 minutes as a continuous conversation of approximately 100 to 200 messages. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to cancel. Reply HELP for help, or email olivia.fitzpatrick@childmind.org. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

What the program is

The Child Mind Institute is testing an automated text-message interviewer. This is a pilot test of the software, not a research study: its purpose is to find out whether the system works reliably before any research data is collected.

Testers are recruited through the Prolific research platform. Each tester is given a short written persona describing a fictional parent and a fictional child, and answers a standardized mental health screening questionnaire in character as that fictional parent. No tester is asked about their own child, their own family, or their own mental health.

What messages look like

Hello! I am an AI assistant from the Child Mind Institute, messaging you to ask some questions about the child described in the persona you were given, as part of the DASH Mental Health Screener. The first few questions ask about the child's physical health.

Your phone number

Phone numbers are used only to conduct the conversation. We do not sell, rent, trade, or share mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in data with third parties, and they are never used for marketing. A number reaches us as an unavoidable part of receiving a text message; it is converted to an irreversible cryptographic hash on arrival and the number itself is never written to our database.
